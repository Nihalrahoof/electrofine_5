## Fonts

Manrope and DM Sans are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Their licence notices are bundled in `assets/fonts/`.

- Manrope: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
- DM Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans

The downloaded TTF fonts were converted to WOFF without changing their design. Georgia is a system font and is not distributed in this package.

## Brand and icons

The ELECTROFINE wordmark treatment, three-line brand symbol, and favicon were created for this website concept. Interface arrows and decorative marks are text/CSS, without an external icon dependency. The supplied reference screenshots were used for art direction only and are not redistributed in the website package.

## Product concept illustrations
The six `product-*.svg` images are original vector illustrations created for this concept. They do not depict a specific manufacturer, model or rated configuration. Team cards use generic portrait silhouettes until approved staff photographs are available. The home hero reuses the credited power-lines photograph.
