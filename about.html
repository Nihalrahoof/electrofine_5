01 — OUR FOUNDATION

Engineering is our discipline.
Reliability is our legacy.

Established over 27 years ago, ELECTROFINE Electrical Engineers and Contractors began with a fundamental commitment to electrical safety and execution excellence.

Today, as a fully licensed electrical contracting firm, we deliver high-voltage and low-voltage systems across commercial, public-sector, and utility infrastructure.

Our growth has been measured in capability: from local commercial electrification to the complex requirements of utility-scale projects.
