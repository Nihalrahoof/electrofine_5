Privacy notice

This website is a static company presentation. It does not create accounts, use advertising trackers, or set analytics cookies.

Project enquiries

The enquiry form prepares information you choose to provide, such as your name, email, company, phone, project location, and requirements. In preview mode, the form creates a text file on your device. No information is submitted to ELECTROFINE.

When a company email address is configured, the form can open a draft in your email application. You review and send it yourself. Your email provider then handles the message under its own terms. This site does not store a copy of your form entries on a server or in browser storage.

Hosting and technical information

The hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website. Refer to the hosting provider’s privacy policy for details.

Images and fonts

Images and fonts are included locally in the website package. The website does not require external font or image requests for its core presentation.

Questions and updates

Use the verified business contact details displayed on the Contact page once they are available. This notice should be reviewed when live contact details, analytics, third-party forms, or other services are added.
